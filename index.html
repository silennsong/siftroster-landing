Frequently Asked Questions

How is CreatorSift different from generic influencer databases?

Static creator databases rely on outdated scrapes and vanity follower counts. CreatorSift runs fresh discovery per brief, checks recent non-pinned video medians, evaluates actual video context with AI against your client persona, and verifies contact confidence with source evidence.

Can I use CreatorSift for multiple clients as a solo agency?

Yes. Boutique Agency and Scale Agency tiers separate persona rules, candidate pools, and outreach suppression lists by client workspace—while still protecting against duplicate outreach across platforms.

Why do you keep a human-in-the-loop review step?

Full auto-send bots ruin domain reputation and send embarrassing pitches to mismatched creators. CreatorSift automates the 90% tedious work (discovery, metric gating, AI scoring, email verification, and draft writing) while giving you a fast batch sign-off board before anything is sent.
